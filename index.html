About us

Welcome to Hello Gorgeous!!

We are a professional hair salon located in the unique village of Waterdown. At Hello Gorgeous our goal is to make your visit with us the best appointment of your week. Our stylists are all trained, certified, licensed, and most importantly talented and enthusiastic.

We are dedicated to creating the most positive salon experience for our clients, and to making you look and feel gorgeous. Ongoing training is also part of our mission: as professional stylists, we are always learning the latest techniques.
